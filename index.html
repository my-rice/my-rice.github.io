research

Four threads, one loop.

From raw sensing to reliable, real-time dexterous control, structured so learning stays sample-efficient and planning stays cheap.

01

Structured world models

Non-reconstructive, joint-embedding predictive models (JEPA) that learn latent dynamics directly from proprioception and touch, instead of spending capacity reconstructing pixels.

JEPAlatent dynamicsself-supervised
02

Low-rank LPV dynamics

A locally linear dynamics head with low-rank factorization A=I+UVᵀ, B=PQᵀ. LPV structure stays expressive without a quadratic parameter blowup, and exposes the matrices for fast gradient-based planning.

LPVlow-rankKoopmancontrol
03

Dexterous manipulation

Multi-fingered, tendon-driven hands (17-DoF OrcaHand) reaching and grasping from state alone, fusing proprioception with tactile sensing for contact-rich tasks.

OrcaHandtactilecontact-rich
04

Sim-to-real & hardware

Bridging simulation and a real Franka Panda + OrcaHand stack with domain randomization, asymmetric actor-critic RL, and agentic hyperparameter tuning to survive the reality gap.

sim-to-realPPOdomain randomization

about

I am a first-year PhD student in robotics and machine learning at IDSIA in Lugano, Switzerland. My research asks how robots can build internal models of contact-rich interaction that are efficient enough to learn from limited data and structured enough to plan with in real time.
